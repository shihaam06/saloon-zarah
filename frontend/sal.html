Common Questions

Everything salon owners ask before getting started.

Have questions about setup, billing, or security? We've got answers.

How long does it take to set up my salon on Kangro SAL?
Under 10 minutes. Just create an account, add your main services (haircut, color, facial, spa) with prices, and connect your Instagram. Our team also provides free WhatsApp onboarding to help you upload your full service menu.
Do I need a GST number to use Kangro SAL?
No. Kangro SAL works seamlessly for both unregistered small studios (generating regular retail receipts) and GST-registered salons (with automated CGST/SGST and SAC 999721 codes). You can turn GST on or off with a single toggle.
Is my salon and customer data safe?
100% safe and confidential. Your customer phone numbers, visit histories, and revenue data belong entirely to your salon. We never sell or share client data. All databases are protected by encrypted cloud infrastructure.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation penalties. You can cancel with 1 click directly from your dashboard or by messaging us on WhatsApp.
Does Kangro SAL work if my salon doesn't have a website?
Yes! Over 80% of our salon partners do not have a website. Kangro works through your existing Instagram page (via our AI receptionist) and provides a direct booking link you can put in your Instagram bio or WhatsApp profile.
Does the Instagram AI receptionist support Hindi or regional languages?
Yes. Kangro's AI understands natural Indian conversational style — including Hindi, Hinglish, and regional terms (e.g. "bhaiya kal 4 baje haircut ka slot milega kya?" or "keratin treatment ka kitna charges hai?") and replies politely and accurately.
